# Opportunity list: `niwot`

Follow-up experiments and pipeline improvements that could add gain on this host
(see [`host_niwot.md`](host_niwot.md)). Each item says what to run and what result would change a verdict.
When an item is done, strike it through and link the write-up, rather than deleting it.

Rough priority: **H** is likely to move a suite geomean or fix a pipeline blind spot, **M** could firm up or
slightly improve an existing result, **L** is a curiosity or diagnostic.

| 7 | ~~H~~ | **End-of-Phase-4 baseline re-check:** one or two extra `-O3` reps after confirmation, flagging a baseline offset beyond some threshold. | `729.abc_r`'s calibration sampled about 0.5% low (confirmed by re-measure), inflating every delta and the knowledge-table means. | 8 | ~~H~~ | **Don't let a low-confidence corpus shape exclude whole flag families.** When the corpus margin between primary and alternative shape is narrow, union the two shapes' plausible candidates, or fall back to local `deep-cpu`. | sqlite and abc both excluded LTO and PGO on a ~4-5 point corpus margin from another machine. `734.vpr_r` (memory-bound 45.4%) proves the cost: LTO + PGO, excluded by the shape, measured +14.65% on top of the mine's winner (#20), sqlite (39.7%) hid PGO +9.65%, abc (38.2%) hid `-flto` +3.10%, and gcc (45.2%) hid LTO + PGO +10.87%. All four memory-bound benchmarks here gained. The exception is `777.zstd_r` (64.0%, the clearest margin): its check found `-flto` flat and PGO -1.7%, so the exclusion was right there. The exclusion looks wrong at moderate margins and fine at a clear majority. **Merged 2026-10-22 (PR #48). First real run, `707.ntest_r` re-mine (2026-10-22): the gate fired at 40.6% and the mine found +30.95%, against +14.27% before.** |

| 26 | **H** | **`782.lbm_r` ratios swing 2x between trials on this host.** The stopped first mine (experiment 151, trials 661-671) measured `-O3` at 6.32, 3.02, 4.15, 4.24, 4.26 and `-fprefetch-loop-arrays` at 3.04, 4.75, 5.46; wall-clock time tracked the ratio (about 24 min at 6.3, 50 min at 3.0). | Blocks fprate mining: a tight 3-rep baseline CI against candidates that swing this much gives false verdicts. **Investigated 2026-10-23** ([note](lbm_swing_investigation.2026-10-23.md)): a system-wide memory-bandwidth state, about 45 or about 22.5 GB/s, that often flips between runs. lbm's ceiling is a very repeatable 6.33; slow runs drop as low as 3.02. The probe-and-rerun guards merged 2026-10-23 (PR #54), but the guarded mine (experiment 165) still got a throttled baseline: every probe passed, the throttle began mid-run, and all three calibration reps were slow (4.06-4.35). At 8 copies the fast state is the same (6.43-6.47) and the slow one only 18% down (5.22-5.35), still two states ([note](lbm_swing_investigation.2026-10-23.md#the-guarded-mine-and-8-copies-instead-of-16-2026-10-23)). **2026-10-24: fprate mining moved to another host; niwot mines intspeed instead.** |
